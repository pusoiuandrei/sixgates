# Corpus of primary sources (downloaded 2026-11-02 from berkshirehathaway.com and fs.blog)

txt/1977.txt ... txt/2024.txt : Berkshire Hathaway Chairman's letters (Warren Buffett) for each year (the file year = the year the letter reports on; e.g. 1986.txt is the letter in the 1986 annual report, published early 1987).
txt/2025.txt : the letter for 2025, published February 2026 (Greg Abel's first letter as CEO).
txt/ownman.txt : Berkshire Owner's Manual (1996, updated), the 15 owner-related business principles.
txt/buffett_florida1998.txt : Buffett's talk at the University of Florida, October 1998 (transcript).
txt/munger_usc1994_worldly_wisdom.txt : Munger, "A Lesson on Elementary, Worldly Wisdom", USC Business School, 1994.
txt/munger_harvard1995_misjudgment.txt : Munger, "The Psychology of Human Misjudgment", Harvard, 1995 (fs.blog transcript).
txt/buffett_50years_2014.txt and txt/munger_50years_2014.txt : the two 2014 special letters "Berkshire, Past, Present and Future".
txt/brk_2024_annual_report.txt : the full 2024 annual report (10-K plus letter and other sections).
Not in the corpus (verify only via web search if needed): the Buffett Partnership letters 1957-1970, Superinvestors of Graham-and-Doddsville (1984), the 2010 FCIC interview, Munger's USC 2007 commencement, Poor Charlie's Almanack, annual meeting transcripts.
